what lynxr does

you send lynxr a link to a short-form video — TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube. lynxr downloads that video, transcribes what is said in it, looks at its frames, and works out the format underneath it: the hook, the beats, the order they come in. it then rewrites that format as a new script for a company you are making content for.

the point is the structure, not the words. lynxr is built to take the shape of a video and put a different subject through it, which is a normal and long-standing thing for creators to do by hand.
