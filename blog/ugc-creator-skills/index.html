What skills does a UGC creator need?

A UGC creator needs seven skills: scripting, writing hooks, talking naturally on camera, getting clean light and sound, editing, reading a brief, and communicating clearly with a brand. None of them needs talent to start. Each one improves fastest with a short, repeatable practice you can do at home.

Published 3 November 2026

Which skills matter most when you are just starting?

Start with scripting and on-camera delivery, because every video you make depends on both and you can practise them with a phone and a product you own.

Light, sound and editing matter too, but they are partly solved by setup: a window, a quiet room and a simple edit get you a long way. Writing and delivery are not solved by setup. They are the parts a brand is paying a creator for, so put your first hours there. How do you get better at writing scripts and hooks?

Write a lot of short scripts on a fixed structure, because a structure shows you exactly which part of a weak script is weak.

Use the parts in the parts of a UGC video script as your frame: a hook, a setup, a few beats, a turn and a close. When a draft feels flat, check each part in order. Often the hook promises one thing and the middle delivers another, and you will only catch that when you are looking for it.

  • Pick one object you own and write a script for it every day for a week, so you stop relying on the product being interesting.
  • Write the hook last, after you know what the video pays off, so the opening points at something real.
  • Read each script out loud before filming, because a line that is hard to say will sound written on camera.
  • Keep a file of openings you liked in other videos and note why they worked, without copying the words.

How do you sound natural on camera when you are nervous?

Learn the beats rather than the exact words, then talk to one person you know through the lens, so the delivery sounds like speech instead of reading.

Word-for-word memorising makes your eyes drift while you search for the next line. Knowing that beat two is where you open the box and beat three is where you show the inside lets you say it a little differently each take, which is how people talk. Film several takes in a row without stopping to watch them, because the third or fourth take is where you start to relax.

How much do you need to know about light, sound and editing?

You need enough to make footage that is evenly lit, clearly heard and cut tight, and you can learn that in a few practice sessions.

Face a window, film in a room with soft furnishings, and keep your phone close enough that your voice is the loudest thing in the clip. In the edit, cut every pause before a line and every breath after it, then watch it back once with the sound off to check the pictures still tell the story. Why do reading briefs and communicating count as skills?

They decide whether a good video is the right video, and they are what makes a brand comfortable sending you the next job.

A brief holds the lines you must say, the claims you must not make, and the format the brand has in mind. Missing one of those can mean refilming, however good the take was. Read it twice: once for the idea and once with a pen, marking what is fixed and what is yours to decide. How to turn a brand brief into a script walks through that markup step by step.

Communication is mostly confirming things before they become problems. When a brief is unclear, ask one specific question rather than guessing, such as, [NAME], should the product be in frame for the hook, or only from the setup onwards? Send the delivery date back in writing when you accept a job, and say early if anything will move it.

How do you show these skills before anyone has hired you?

Make a small set of sample videos that each show a different skill, so whoever views them can judge your range quickly.

Write one around a strong hook, one that shows a product demo with clean light and sound, and one that follows a mock brief you wrote yourself. That last one proves you can work to instructions, which a polished video alone does not show.
