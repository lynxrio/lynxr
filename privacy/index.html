privacy

last updated 17 august 2026

lynxr is run by Lynx Media Group LLC (“we”, “us”). this page says what we collect, why we collect it, who else can see it, and how to have it deleted. it is written to be read rather than to be thorough-looking.

the wait list

if you enter your email on the home page we store three things: your email address, the date and time you entered it, and a short label for the link you arrived from (for example a campaign tag, or the site that referred you — we store only the hostname, never the full referring address).

we use it for one thing: to email you once, when lynxr launches. we do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone outside the processors listed below, and we will not add you to any other mailing list. every email we send will have a one-click unsubscribe.

accounts

if you have a lynxr account we store the email address you signed up with. passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored hashed — we never see them and cannot recover them.

what you put into lynxr

when you use the app we store what you create in it: the companies you add, the video links you paste, any notes you write, and the scripts produced from them. this is yours, and other creators using lynxr cannot see it — access is enforced by the database, not just by the interface.

lynx media group staff can see the video links you paste. we collect them so we can learn which formats are working across the creators we work with. your notes, your companies and your scripts are not used this way. we are telling you because you would reasonably assume otherwise.

who else processes it

we use these services to run lynxr. they process data on our instructions and for no other purpose:

  • Supabase — database and sign-in
  • Anthropic — the AI that reads a video and writes the script. transcripts, captions and the text of a company’s website are sent to it
  • Apify — collecting publicly posted video data
  • Google — a copy of the wait list is mirrored into a private Google Sheet so we can read it
  • GitHub — hosts this site, and keeps standard server logs including IP addresses

how long we keep it

wait list addresses are deleted once we have launched and emailed you, or sooner if you ask.

account data is kept for as long as your account is open. there is no delete button in the app yet, so closing an account is a request: email us and we will delete the account and everything in it — your companies, your saved links, your scripts — within 30 days. copies can persist in encrypted backups for a short period after that before they age out.

your choices

you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. email lynxrnetwork@gmail.com and we will action it. you do not need to give a reason, and it costs you nothing.

if you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

cookies

we do not use tracking or advertising cookies, and there is no analytics on this site. the app stores a sign-in token in your browser so you are not asked to log in on every page — that is required for it to work at all, and it is not used to track you.
