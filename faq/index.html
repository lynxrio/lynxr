questions and answers

what lynxr is, what it does with a link, what it keeps, and how to get started — sign‑up is open. if something here is not answered, the privacy policy and the terms go further.

what is lynxr?

lynxr turns a short‑form video into a script. you send it a public link to a TikTok or Instagram video; it watches the video, transcribes what is said, works out the format underneath — the hook, the beats, the order they come in — and rewrites that format as a script for the company you are making content for. it is made by lynxr LLC, a partner company of Lynx Media Group, the UGC agency.

who is lynxr for?

lynxr is for creators who make short‑form video for brands, and the people who brief them. if your job is to look at a video that worked and produce something in that shape for a different company, that is the job lynxr does. you must be 18 or older to use it.

what does “format” mean in lynxr?

a format is the shape of a video rather than its subject: the hook and the job it is doing, the beats and their order, the turn where the video becomes something else, and the close. two videos about completely different products can run the same format. that is why a format travels between brands when a topic does not — copying the topic gets you a video about somebody else's product, copying the format gets you the thing that made it work.

how is lynxr different from asking ChatGPT to write a script?

lynxr starts from a specific video rather than from a prompt. it downloads the video you chose, transcribes what is actually said in it, takes frames from it, and works out the format that video is running before it writes anything, so the script you get has the shape of a video that already worked. a general chat assistant is writing from a description of a video it has not seen. lynxr also writes one script per company you picked, from that same single read of the video.

what happens to a link i paste into lynxr?

a link you paste into lynxr is queued against your own account, then a worker downloads the video, transcribes the audio, takes a cover frame and a handful of stills, extracts the format, and writes one script per company you sent it to. the script, the transcript and the details of the source video are saved to your own row in our database so the video appears in your library. nothing is posted anywhere and nothing is shared with other users. the full detail is in the privacy policy.

can other people see the scripts i make?

no. what you make in lynxr is yours, and other creators cannot see it — that is enforced by the database rather than by the interface, so having an account is not the same as being able to read somebody else's work. nothing you make is published anywhere. the detail is in the privacy policy.

which platforms does lynxr work with?

lynxr works with TikTok and Instagram — a TikTok video or short link, an Instagram reel or post. a link on any other host, YouTube and Facebook included for now, is refused before anything is downloaded, and refusing it costs you nothing from your allowance.

does lynxr work with Instagram reels and TikTok?

yes. an Instagram reel, an Instagram post with video, a TikTok and the vm.tiktok.com short form are all accepted. YouTube and Facebook links are not accepted right now. lynxr is built for short‑form video, so a short one is the case it handles best; a longer video still works and simply takes longer.

do i have to upload a file or connect my accounts?

no — lynxr never asks you to upload a file or connect an account. there is no file upload anywhere in lynxr — a public link is the only thing it takes. it never asks for your password to any platform, it is not connected to your accounts, and it never posts anything on your behalf.

does lynxr post anything to my accounts?

no. lynxr is not connected to any of your accounts, it never asks for your password to any platform, and it has no way to post anything anywhere. the only thing it hands back is your own script, in your own library.

how long does a script take?

a pasted link is usually picked up within a couple of seconds, and a short video — around a minute long or less — is typically written within a minute or two of that. a longer video takes longer, and the first script after the worker has been idle is slower than the ones after it because the transcription model has to load. we do not show a countdown, because a countdown that reaches zero while you are still waiting is worse than no countdown.

can one link become more than one script?

yes. pick several companies when you send the link and you get one script per company. the video is only downloaded, transcribed and read once no matter how many companies you send it to, so the second and third scripts cost roughly a second each rather than a full pass.

what happens if lynxr cannot open my video?

you get a plain sentence saying why — private, deleted, age‑restricted, blocked in our region, or an unsupported link — and it costs you nothing from your allowance. where the problem could not be fixed by trying again, no retry button is offered, because offering one would only waste your time.

who owns the scripts lynxr writes?

you do. the scripts are yours to use, edit, publish and be paid for; lynxr claims no ownership of them and needs no licence to publish them, because it never publishes them. one honest caveat, which is a fact about the law rather than a term we set: a machine‑generated script may not be protected by copyright the way something you wrote yourself is. the full wording is in the terms.

what does lynxr cost?

lynxr is free to start: every account includes 25 scripts, with no card on file and nothing to cancel. lynxr pro — $24.99 a month with a fair‑use ceiling — is on the pricing page. nothing starts billing you because you left an account open.

how do i get access to lynxr?

sign‑up is open to everyone. paste a video link on the lynxr homepage and create your account when you send it — every new account includes 25 free scripts, no card required.

who makes lynxr?

lynxr is made by lynxr LLC, a partner company of Lynx Media Group LLC, the UGC agency in Allston, Massachusetts founded by Gawin Hsu and Junsa Hwang. lynxr grew out of the agency's own work, which is why it is aimed at the job the agency actually does.

is lynxr the Honeywell LYNXR alarm panel or the Lynx R headset?

no. lynxr (lynxr.io) is a script tool for short‑form video creators, made by lynxr LLC. it is not related to the Honeywell or Ademco LYNXR security control panels, the Lynx R-1 or R2 mixed reality headsets, or lynxr.com.

how much does lynxr cost?

starting is free: every account includes 25 scripts for the life of the account, with no card required. lynxr pro is $24.99 a month and gives you unlimited scripts under a fair-use limit of 150 in any rolling 30 days and 30 in any 24 hours. tax is added at checkout where it applies, so the total may be higher depending on where you are. lynxr max, $74.99 a month, adds the content coach and post tracking, and is not on sale yet.

how do I cancel lynxr pro?

cancel any time from the plan page in the app: sign in, open plan, choose cancel subscription, and confirm on stripe's page. you can also email hello@lynxr.io or reply to your stripe receipt and it is cancelled for you. cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for, so you keep pro until then, and there is no pro‑rata refund for the unused part. there is a 14-day money-back guarantee on your first payment, and a refund ends access straight away. nothing you have written is deleted when you go back to the free tier.

who takes the payment for lynxr?

Stripe is the merchant of record for lynxr pro. Stripe takes the payment, issues the invoice, collects any tax that is due where you are, and issues refunds. lynxr never sees or stores your card details.
