# lynxr brand

### The guy (logo)
- A plain white circle with two black `^^` eyes. Full spec and assets: LOGO-HANDOFF.md.
- **Colours, on every logo from here on (owner, 2026-11-25):** body soft white `#f2f2f0`, eyes near-black `#141414`.
  Black and white, just softened enough not to glare. No tint, no other colours. The wordmark beside him matches.
- At rest he's still. He comes alive on hover, focus, tap, or while lynxr is working:
  the circle melts into a soft blob, breathes, and the `^^` blink into `--`.
- Core idea: **the eyes stay, the shape shifts.** The eyes never change; only the body does.
- The logo is serious and black and white. The playfulness lives in the motion and the
  small interactions, not in loud colors or a mascot costume.

### What the name means
- **Lynx = sharp sight.** "Lynx-eyed" means seeing what others miss. In Greek myth,
  Lynceus could see through walls. The logo is literally just the eyes.
- **Lynx is a constellation.** It was named in the 1600s, and it's so faint you'd
  "need the eyes of a lynx" to see it. That's the product: the pattern is already
  there, but only sharp eyes catch it.
- **lynxr ≈ "linker".** You paste a link and lynxr connects the dots between viral videos.
- The `^^` can also be read as lynx ears. Keep that a quiet nod, never a cat logo.
- It ties back to the agency, Lynx Media Group.

### The core metaphor (use in copy, not as a theme)
- **A viral video is a shooting star:** bright, random, not repeatable.
- **A format is a constellation:** a fixed pattern you can find again and steer by.
- **lynxr turns shooting stars into constellations:** one lucky hit becomes a repeatable system.
- In the app: a creator's posts are stars. When lynxr spots a pattern, it draws the
  constellation (their format that works). Their goal is the north star.

### Copy lines we like
- "lynxr sees what makes it work."
- "Stop chasing shooting stars. Find your constellation."
- "Find the pattern only sharp eyes see."

### Guardrails (important)
- **No space theme.** No rockets, astronauts, planets, galaxy gradients, or cartoon
  stars. Avoid "to the moon" (crypto/meme-stock hype). The metaphor lives in the
  writing and in a few small details, never as the look of the site.
- Don't add craters, glow or a face to the guy. He stays flat: circle + `^^`.
- The site isn't monochrome. Dark background, white guy, a few warm accents.
  Vibe reference: heyclicky.com (cozy, interactive, easter eggs, music).

### Easter egg ideas (only build when asked)
- Loading state: the guy cycles through moon phases, then comes back full.
- Late at night (local time), a few faint stars appear in the shape of the Lynx
  constellation. Hovering connects them, with a tiny line: "only sharp eyes see this one."
- Poke him enough times and he briefly grows lynx ears.
