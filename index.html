how it works

three steps, and only one of them is yours

  1. 1

    paste a link

    a video you already believe in — yours or anyone's. there is nothing to upload and no account to connect.

  2. 2

    lynxr reads it back

    it watches the video, transcribes what is said, and works out the format underneath — not the topic.

  3. 3

    you get a script

    that same format, rewritten for the company you are making content for. send one link to three brands and you get three scripts.
