About lynxr

lynxr is your content creator buddy, made by lynxr LLC, a partner company of Lynx Media Group, the UGC agency. You paste a public TikTok or Instagram link; it works out the format underneath the video — the hook, the beats, the turn and the close — and rewrites it as a script for the brand you make content for.

Who makes lynxr

lynxr is made by lynxr LLC, a partner company of Lynx Media Group LLC, the UGC agency in Allston, Massachusetts founded by Gawin Hsu and Junsa Hwang. lynxr grew out of the agency's own work, which is why it is aimed at the job the agency actually does: looking at a video that worked and producing something in that shape for a different company.

The agency's own site is Lynx Media Group.

How lynxr and Lynx Media Group fit together

They are partner companies doing two halves of one job. Lynx Media Group runs short-form video campaigns for brands, with creators. lynxr LLC makes lynxr for the creator's side of that work: writing the script. If you are a creator who needs a script, you are in the right place. If you are a brand looking for creators to make video for you, that is the agency's work, at lynxmediagroup.org.

What lynxr does

You send lynxr a public link to a TikTok or Instagram video. It downloads the video, transcribes what is said, works out the format — the hook, the beats and their order, the turn and the close — and writes one script per company you pick, reading the video only once. It never asks for a platform password and never posts anything on your behalf. Every account includes 25 free scripts, no card required; pricing has the rest.
