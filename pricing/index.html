pricing

last updated 25 august 2026

lynxr turns a video you paste into a script written for a company you choose. there are two ways to use it: a free tier you never have to pay for, and one paid tier.

free

$0. twenty-five scripts, for the lifetime of the account.

  • no card, and no trial that turns into a subscription.
  • the twenty-five are a lifetime total, not twenty-five a month — they do not refill.
  • everything you write on the free tier stays yours and stays readable after the five are used.

lynxr pro

$24.99 a month, billed monthly in advance. unlimited scripts, subject to the fair-use ceiling below.

  • you never buy scripts one at a time.
  • cancel any time. cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • 14-day money-back on your first payment — see the refund policy.

fair use

pro is unlimited in the sense that you never buy scripts one at a time and you will not run out doing the work it is for. it is not unmetered. pro includes up to 300 scripts in any rolling 30-day period and up to 30 in any 24 hours.

the 30-day window rolls continuously — it does not reset on your billing date. each script counts against you for 30 days from the moment it is written, and room comes back as older ones age out.

for scale: a creator posting once a day writes about 30 scripts a month, so this is roughly ten times heavy use. it exists to stop automated or shared-account use, not to ration normal work.

if you reach either limit nothing is deleted and everything already written stays yours; new scripts resume as earlier ones age out. if you have a real need for more, ask — we would rather raise your limit than have you work around it.

tax, currency and who you pay

prices are in USD. tax is added at checkout where it is required, so the total you are shown before paying may be higher than $24.99 depending on where you are.

Paddle.com is our merchant of record. that means Paddle takes the payment, issues the invoice, handles tax, and issues any refund — your card statement will say Paddle, not lynxr.

if a payment fails

Paddle retries a failed payment for a few days before access drops back to the free tier. nothing you have already written is deleted.

the refund policy and the terms of service both form part of this.
