pricing

last updated 21 september 2026

lynxr turns a video you paste into a script written for a company you choose. there are two ways to use it: a free tier you never have to pay for, and one paid tier. a second paid tier, lynxr max, is announced but not on sale yet.

free

$0. 3 scripts in any rolling 7 days.

  • no card, and no trial that turns into a subscription.
  • each script frees up 7 days after you write it, so the free tier never runs out for good.
  • everything you write on the free tier stays yours and stays readable, whatever plan you are on.

lynxr pro

$24.99 a month, billed monthly in advance. unlimited scripts, subject to the fair-use ceiling below.

  • you never buy scripts one at a time.
  • cancel any time. cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • 14-day money-back on your first payment — see the refund policy.

lynxr max — coming soon

$74.99 a month, when it opens. it is not on sale yet.

  • everything in pro, with a higher fair-use ceiling: up to 300 scripts in any rolling 30-day period and up to 40 in any 24 hours.
  • post tracking, and a coach that reads how your videos did and suggests what to make next. neither is built yet, which is why max cannot be bought.

fair use

pro is unlimited in the sense that you never buy scripts one at a time and you will not run out doing the work it is for. it is not unmetered. pro includes up to 150 scripts in any rolling 30-day period and up to 30 in any 24 hours.

the 30-day window rolls continuously — it does not reset on your billing date. each script counts against you for 30 days from the moment it is written, and room comes back as older ones age out.

for scale: a creator posting once a day writes about 30 scripts a month, so this is roughly five times heavy use. it exists to stop automated or shared-account use, not to ration normal work.

if you reach either limit nothing is deleted and everything already written stays yours; new scripts resume as earlier ones age out. if you have a real need for more, ask — we would rather raise your limit than have you work around it.

tax, currency and who you pay

prices are in USD. tax is added at checkout where it is required, so the total you are shown before paying may be higher than $24.99 depending on where you are.

Stripe is our merchant of record. that means Stripe takes the payment, works out and adds any tax, issues the receipt, and handles refunds and disputes.

if a payment fails

Stripe retries a failed payment before access drops back to the free tier; pro stays on while it does. nothing you have already written is deleted.

the refund policy and the terms of service both form part of this.
