Taxonomy and decision rules
Beats have role, start_s, end_s and modality; intervals must be ordered and nonoverlapping. Hook boundaries need both start_s and end_s. Product-entry/reveal are seconds from video start. Pace is a subjective slow/medium/fast category, not cuts per second. “none” is explicitly observed absence; null is unknown. The visual delivery label means no speech, not merely a visually interesting video. Each independent revision is preserved; it does not silently replace another rater.
