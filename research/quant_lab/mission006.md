# Mission 006 implementation contract

Extend PR #13 locally, retaining its existing real sample and production isolation. Add a second, separately attributed source: Grigorii Tvorogov (2026), *Educational Instagram Reels: Coded Dataset of 300 Reels from 21 Accounts, with Codebook and Technical Report*, version 1.0, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23301344](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23301344), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source workbook and report were downloaded from the public Zenodo record, with provider checksums verified. No video, audio or thumbnail download is authorized by that license decision.

This experiment-local projection adds source identity, publication precision, coding-date precision, approximate age in calendar days, original counter strings, full source cell mapping and third-party human annotations to existing catalog records. It does not modify shared/production schemas or the observation ledger. For the new source, `published_at` is an ISO **date**, explicitly labeled day precision with unspecified timezone, never an invented UTC timestamp. Metric observation is a coding **date**, not an exact instant. Workbook Video ID is a study row identity, not a platform-native numeric post ID. Reel shortcode remains the source-linked identity. Rounded K/M counters are retained and labeled approximate.

The two sources remain separate in catalog filters, summaries and creator references: their counter definitions, collection methods and sampling frames differ. No pooled performance ranking. A new research view provides at least six actual annotated examples in deterministic within-account pairs and source-specific descriptive structure comparisons. Human annotations remain attributed third-party judgments, never caption regex output or our own viewing. Full beats, reveal timing, product-entry timing and objective cut rates are unavailable.

Same-account references may use at least four OTHER posts from the same source in a predefined calendar-age band (0–2, 3–7, 8–30, 31–90, 91+ days). Ratios to their median are descriptive, use contemporaneously coded sample outcomes and coarse ages, and are not a pre-publication baseline, holdout, prediction or causal estimate. Unknown age and sparse references return unavailable. The default research tables will show within-account coverage and confounding, not significance claims. Null observations and literal feature category `None` remain distinct.

Data, receipts, full record exports and record screenshots remain outside Git. The application is GET-only and loopback-bound with no media requests or connected API. The additional source is fixed, version-pinned and digest-checked; there is no general downloader. Tests exercise the actual downloaded workbook through an opt-in offline verification command. No new synthetic catalog or fixture project is introduced.

The six examples displayed by the application are actual workbook rows. Their source-linked details include original cell values, metric/coding dates, unknowns, annotation provenance and rights. Selection is deterministic: three largest same-account age-band groups with at least five posts, one group per account; inspect the lowest and highest source views within each group. This intentionally selects extremes for inspection, not an unbiased test of hook efficacy.

These contrasts show that shared broad structure labels do not determine reach. They do not identify why an individual post did better. The first original Instagram page was opened without login: its account and August 3 publication label matched the linked workbook row. A signup prompt appeared; no login, playback validation, frame extraction, media download or current counter ingestion occurred. The other five source links were verified in local detail views, not independently replayed. Future live availability is not guaranteed.

**Creator context changes the descriptive comparison.** Human Fast pacing: n=44, seven accounts, median source views 831,500. Slow: n=219, 19 accounts, median 31,800. Those pooled medians differ about 26×. Median leave-one-out same-account age-band ratios are approximately **1.011× versus 0.983×**, on 32 versus 170 eligible rows. Only eight of 21 accounts vary in pacing. This is an illustrative confounding check, not an adjusted causal effect, significance result or successful prediction. The reference ratio is unavailable for sparse age bands; 230/300 rows meet the four-OTHER-post rule. Broad calendar-age bands and approximate counters remain substantial limitations. Ratios near one are partly a consequence of within-account centering, especially for nearly constant account traits; this is not an estimated pacing effect. No exact-age historical baseline is declared eligible.

Hooks are multi-label: group totals overlap. Same-account variation compares sorted category sets, not the order of labels in a cell. CTA `None` means an absent CTA, not a missing annotation. Attention-retention-potential scores are the author's judgments; they must not be described as measured audience retention. All original factual/coded workbook fields can be inspected under the detail disclosure; no unobserved beat timeline, reveal time, product integration or cuts-per-second is inferred.

## First prospective measurements

This is an operational proposal, not new Schedule B acceptance criteria.

1. The Lynxr research owner obtains scoped research permission from **one willing creator** for metadata/analytics snapshots, use/retention and withdrawal; separately authorize owned footage or supplied annotations if needed. No private roster or credentials are assumed. An owner-authorized upload/export is the dependency; do not request a production key.
2. On receipt, take the **first actual observation now** from that creator's permitted analytics screenshot/export: post URL/native ID, source counter name/definition and displayed precision, publication timestamp/zone, actual reading time and local receipt time. Keep the original permission and evidence privately outside Git. If the source only gives an export generation time, record that separately and do not backdate its metrics. Existing lifetime readings are descriptive initialization, not fixed-age baseline outcomes.
3. Before the next posts/outcomes, freeze the consenting roster, complete listing rule, target metric and proposed **48h ±60min** observation window, missing/withdrawal policy and a prediction/annotation receipt in an existing owner-approved private system with independent timestamping. A local file hash alone is insufficient. No model or paid timestamping service is required to start observing.
4. Ask the creator to supply analytics readings at that scheduled age for **every** eligible new Reel, including low-count posts and unsuccessful attempts. Preserve zero, hidden, denied, unavailable and deleted statuses separately, subject to deletion rights. Record actual age and whether the reading is in tolerance; do not substitute a later lifetime count when missed.
5. Start with timestamped outcome collection; keep prediction evaluation ineligible until at least four comparable prior other-post outcomes are available before a cutoff and all required timing evidence exists. Expand toward five consenting creators for operational coverage, retaining sparse/no-post creators in accounting. Power/sample-size requirements must come from the actual target and pilot variability, not these exploratory contrasts.
6. Only then implement a separately reviewed official read-only collector, with approved account scopes, free/unbilled access confirmed, current limits and stop-on-denial behavior. The first manual observation route works without that integration. No collector, credential loader or new schema project is part of this increment.

The licensed human annotations already provide a lawful alternative to media processing. For missing beat/reveal/product measurements, request creator-supplied timecoded annotations or creator-owned footage with explicit rights for local viewing, frames/transcripts and research derivatives. Do not download sample media or send footage to a model without those rights. No automated multimodal analysis was performed.
